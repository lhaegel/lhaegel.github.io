CNRS/IN2P3 published a [press release](https://www.in2p3.cnrs.fr/en/cnrsinfo/ricochet-lookout-neutrinonucleus-interaction) about the first science run of the Ricochet experiment. We are using cryogenic detectors to observe extremely faint neutrino interactions, known as coherent elastic neutrino nucleus scattering (CEvNS), and push the frontier of low-energy neutrino detection.
