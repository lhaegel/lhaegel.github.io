I will be at [Alina Noir Salon](https://www.alinanoir.com/) with the artist Sylvain Guibbert on October 18 to present the art piece [Exosunsets](https://sylvainguibbert.com/sci-art/). I will also talk about the book [L'Univers à l'écran au XXIème siècle](https://universalecranxxi.wordpress.com/livre-lunivers-a-lecran-au-xxieme-siecle/) and the [Cosmos Archeology](https://epfl-pavilions.ch/en/exhibitions/cosmos-archaeology) exhibition as an aesthetic rendering of astrophysics.
